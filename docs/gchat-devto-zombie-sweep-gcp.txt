My #AlloyDBSprint entry is published.

Zombie Sweep runs every morning on Cloud Run and keeps its findings in AlloyDB Express. A Gemini agent writes the briefing, and every number in it comes from SQL.

The database pauses after about 15 minutes idle, so a month of daily sweeps uses 7.95-8.56 compute-unit hours, inside the 50 free ones. The whole thing comes to $1.08 a month at list price, $0.09 with the free tier. The most a 1 vCPU Express instance can bill in a month is $89.95, so there is no surprise database bill.
